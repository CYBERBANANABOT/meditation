## Ocean Harmony / Океанская гармония

The second built-in track is a mix of the same Kevin MacLeod recording above (CC BY 4.0) with a real field recording of sea waves:

- Waves: **Oceanwavescrushing.ogg**, recording by **Luftrum** near Røsnæs / Kalundborg Fjord, Denmark.
- Original recording: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oceanwavescrushing.ogg
- Wave recording license: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Music: **Peace of Mind** by **Kevin MacLeod**, CC BY 4.0 — same credit and source as above.
- Modifications: Ocean recording has its ends crossfaded to create a smooth loop; low and high frequencies are filtered and volume is adjusted. Waves and piano are mixed together, limited to avoid clipping, MP3-encoded, and faded in/out.
